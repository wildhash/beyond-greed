# Beyond Greed: 50-Year Roadmap (2025-2075)

## Overview

This roadmap outlines the phased transition from capitalism to post-greed civilization through the systematic deployment of Intention Verification Systems (IVS), Empathy Amplification Networks (EAN), and Collective Intelligence Optimization (CIO).

**Key Principle:** Evolution, not revolution. Each phase builds on the previous, creating irreversible momentum toward systemic transformation.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (2025-2030)

**Theme:** Building trust through transparency and early wins

### 2025-2026: Genesis
**Focus:** Development and initial deployment

- **Q1 2025:** Open source release of Beyond Greed framework
- **Q2 2025:** Form founding community and governance structure
- **Q3-Q4 2025:** Develop IVS, EAN, and CIO prototypes
- **Q1 2026:** Launch first pilot programs

  - Community decision-making tool (CIO)

- ✓ 10,000 active community members
- ✓ 3 functioning pilot programs
- ✓ Open source codebase established
- ✓ Initial academic partnerships formed

### 2027-2028: Proof of Concept
**Focus:** Validate and refine through real-world use

- **2027:** Expand to 50 pilot sites across 20 countries
- **IVS:** Implement in local government procurement (reduce corruption)
- **EAN:** Deploy in conflict mediation and restorative justice
- **CIO:** Integrate into participatory budgeting processes

**Milestones:**
- ✓ Document 30% reduction in corruption where IVS deployed
- ✓ Measurable increase in empathy metrics in EAN communities
- ✓ Decision quality improvements in CIO-enabled processes
- ✓ 100,000 active users

### 2029-2030: Standardization
**Focus:** Create protocols and build momentum

- **2029:** Release v2.0 with standardized APIs and protocols
- Establish interoperability standards
- Create training programs and certifications
- Build coalition of aligned organizations

**Milestones:**
- ✓ 1 million users across 100+ implementations
- ✓ Adopted by 10+ national governments (pilot programs)
- ✓ Academic research validates effectiveness
- ✓ First generation of Beyond Greed practitioners trained

## Critical Success Factors

### 1. Open Source Everything
- All code, all protocols, all data (where privacy permits)
- Prevents capture by existing power structures
- Enables rapid iteration and improvement

### 2. Grassroots + Institutional
- Bottom-up adoption provides legitimacy
- Top-down integration provides scale
- Both necessary for systemic transformation

### 3. Demonstrate Benefits Early
- People adopt what works for them now
- Can't wait for future utopia
- Immediate improvements build momentum

### 4. Antifragility
- Systems must improve through challenges
- Expect resistance and plan for it
- Turn opposition into strength

### 5. Cultural Shift
- Technology alone is insufficient
- Must transform values and worldviews
- Education and art are critical

### 6. Global Coordination
- Some challenges require planetary response
- Systems must work across all contexts
- Unity through diversity, not uniformity

## Risk Scenarios & Mitigations

### Scenario 1: AI Capture
**Risk:** Powerful actors control LLM infrastructure
**Mitigation:** 
- Open source models
- Distributed computing
- Regulatory frameworks

### Scenario 2: Backlash
**Risk:** Coordinated resistance from threatened interests
**Mitigation:**
- Make systems undeniably beneficial
- Offer graceful transition paths
- Build broad coalition

### Scenario 3: Unintended Consequences
**Risk:** Systems create new problems
**Mitigation:**
- Gradual rollout with constant feedback
- Reversibility and escape hatches
- Diverse perspectives in design

### Scenario 4: Technological Stagnation
**Risk:** LLMs don't advance as expected
**Mitigation:**
- Design works with current capabilities
- Parallel development of alternative approaches
- Focus on human wisdom + AI augmentation
